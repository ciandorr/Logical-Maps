# Actuality ⇒ Persistent Comprehension

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Classicism (2023 draft); produced by Andrew Bacon and Cian Dorr; recorded by OpenAI Codex (GPT-6), 17 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Actuality.** There is a true proposition that entails every true proposition.

## Conclusion

- **Persistent Comprehension.** Every relation, including a proposition, is coextensive with a persistent one.

## Proof

If w witnesses Actuality, then $\lambda\bar y\, .\,w\le X[\bar y]$ is coextensive with X. Entailments are necessary when true, so this relation is persistent.
