# Atoms are strong worlds ∧ Self-atoms are atoms ⇒ Self-atoms are strong worlds

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 25 October 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 25 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Atoms are strong worlds.** At each relational type, every atom is a strong world: it necessarily entails every entity of the type or its negation.
- **Self-atoms are atoms.** At each relational type, every self-atom is an atom.

## Conclusion

- **Self-atoms are strong worlds.** At each relational type, every self-atom is a strong world: it necessarily entails every entity of the type or its negation.

## Proof

A self-atom is an atom by the second premise, and so a strong world by the first.
