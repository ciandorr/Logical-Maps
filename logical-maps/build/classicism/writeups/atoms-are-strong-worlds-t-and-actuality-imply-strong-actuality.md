# Atoms are strong worlds (type t) ∧ Actuality ⇒ Strong Actuality

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Cian Dorr, 25 October 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 25 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Atoms are strong worlds (type t).** Every atom is a strong world proposition.
- **Actuality.** There is a true proposition that entails every true proposition.

## Conclusion

- **Strong Actuality.** There is a true strong world proposition: a true proposition that necessarily entails every proposition or its negation.

## Proof

Actuality gives a true atom, which Atoms are strong worlds (type t) makes a strong world: a true strong world.

## Notes

With [[strong-leibniz-t-implies-atoms-are-strong-worlds-t]] and [[strong-leibniz-t-implies-necessary-actuality]] this re-derives [[strong-leibniz-t-implies-strong-actuality]]. The converse fails: Strong Actuality says only that the true atom is strong.
