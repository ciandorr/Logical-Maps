## Conclusion

- **Broad Necessitism.** Every entity of an type is necessarily identical to something.

## Proof

The theorem $\forall x\, .\,\exists y\, .\,y=x$ can be necessitated. Applying CBF yields $\forall x\, .\,\Box\exists y\, .\,y=x$.
