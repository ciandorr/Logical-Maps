# Coalesced sum: substitution arrows over an atomless component with nonstandard arithmetic

<p class='cert'>Model — Source: Misc.; produced by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, in a trawl for cheaply settled verdicts, 29 October 2026 (a variant of Cian Dorr's coalesced-pinned-substitutions-root with one chosen component); recorded by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 29 October 2026.</p>

## Package

- **□Atomlessness.** Necessarily, every possible proposition has a possible proposition strictly below it.
- **Axiom of Infinity (type e).** There are not finitely many individuals. No finite cardinality holds of the universal property, so the domain of the type cannot be exhausted by finitely many steps from the empty property.
- **BF.** The Barcan Formula at every type. The predicate formulation closes the formula schema using lambda abstraction.
- **Two-hat necessities are ND-necessary.** Whatever is two-hat necessary is ND-necessary: the distinctness-preserving modality agrees with its hat.
- **5-necessities are two-hat necessary.** Whatever is 5-necessary is two-hat necessary.
- **Transversal Choice.** Every equivalence relation, at any type including e, has a transversal, that is, a property with exactly one instance in each of its cells.
- **Independence (signature Σ).** No constant of Sigma is a pure operation applied to other constants: for a closed pure term A and distinct constants c, d_1,…,d_n with n ≥ 0, c differs from A applied to the d’s. With n = 0, no constant denotes a pure entity.
- **¬ Necessity of Arithmetic.** Every arithmetical sentence is either necessarily true or necessarily false, given that possibly zero is not a successor and successor is injective on numbers, where the numbers are the finite cardinalities of properties of a type $\sigma$. One sentence for each type $\sigma$ and each arithmetical sentence at $\sigma$.
- **¬ Necessity of Analysis.** Every first-order analytical sentence is either true in every real number structure in every possible world or false in every real number structure in every possible world. One sentence for each type $\sigma$ and each first-order analytical sentence.
- **¬ Arithmetic Necessitism.** Given that necessarily zero is not a successor and successor is injective on numbers, the numbers at $\sigma$ form a rigid property with necessarily distinct instances. One sentence for each type $\sigma$.
- **¬ □BF (type t).** Necessarily, if every proposition necessarily has a property, then necessarily every proposition has it.
- **¬ Truths are ND-necessary.** Every truth is ND-necessary, that is, necessary under the distinctness-preserving modality.
- **¬ Vicinity.** There is a true proposition that entails the possibility of each true proposition.
- **¬ Weakly Inextensible Comprehension.** Every relation, including a proposition, is coextensive with a weakly inextensible one.
- **¬ Boolean Completeness (type t).** Every property of propositions has a greatest lower bound.
- **¬ Truths are 5-necessary.** Every truth is 5-necessary: necessary under every S5 operator.
- **¬ Countable Boolean Completeness.** Every countable property of entities of a relational type has a least upper bound in that type, where a property is countable relative to a type $\sigma$ when it injects into the finite cardinalities of properties of type $\sigma$; one instance for each $\sigma$.
- **¬ B for pure sentences.** The B instance for every closed sentence in the pure language.
- **¬ Witnessed Possibility.** For pure-formulas P with free variables among a finite tuple x, and distinct matching nonlogical constants c, a witness to P entails that P at those constants is possible.
- **¬ Separated Structure.** For each typed nonlogical constant c and closed same-typed terms F,G not containing c, equality after application to c implies equality of F and G.

## Definition

Let $T$ be C together with $\Box$Atomlessness, $\Box I^*_e$ and $\Box\neg$BF at type $t$, a recursively axiomatized theory. $T$ holds in finite-support-monotone-maps, which is atomless, fails BF at type $t$, and has infinitely many individuals and the standard numerals as its numbers at $e$ (the property of being a numeral is pinned down by $\emptyset$); it has one object, so its boxes are idle. So $T$ is consistent, by soundness. $T$ proves $I^*_e$, and so the Peano axioms for its numbers at $e$, so by Gödel's second incompleteness theorem $T+\neg\operatorname{Con}(T)$ is consistent, and by the completeness of action models for C (Classicism, Theorem 3.23: every formula consistent with C holds at some world of some action model) it holds at an object $V_2$ of some action model. The single component is that model cut down to the objects reachable from $V_2$, so that every closed sentence keeps its truth value at each object. The root $W_0$ is built over it exactly as in coalesced-pinned-substitutions-root: an arrow $\langle V,I\rangle$ into each object $V$ of the component for each interpretation $I$ of $\Sigma^+$ there, and an arrow from $W_0$ to itself for each type-preserving map of the constants of $\Sigma^+$. Evaluation point: $W_0$, at its identity arrow. Interpretation of Σ as there. The write-up's argument that the root is a model of C does not use the completeness of the family of components, only that each is an action model of C.

As for interpretation arrows, and the root has besides an arrow to itself for every type-preserving map of the signature's constants to themselves, acting on the root's individuals and composing with the interpretation arrows by precomposition.

As in coalesced-pinned-substitutions-root, intensions over the arrows out of $W_0$ pinned down by a finite set of constants of $\Sigma^+$, whose transports along the arrows into the component land in its domains.

## Arguments

### Holds □Atomlessness.

The arrows out of $W_0$ end at $W_0$ (the maps of the constants) or at objects of the component, and a closed sentence holds at an arrow iff it holds at the identity of its target. At every object of the component Atomlessness holds, by $\Box$Atomlessness at $V_2$, the component being generated from $V_2$. At $W_0$ it holds by the argument atomlessness of coalesced-pinned-substitutions-root, which uses of the components only that $\top\ne\bot$ at each of their objects: a possible $p$ pinned down by $S$ that contains a map is split by a fresh individual constant, and one that contains only arrows into the component by a fresh propositional constant sent to $\top$ or to $\bot$. So $\Box$Atomlessness holds.

### Holds Axiom of Infinity (type e). Fails Necessity of Arithmetic, Necessity of Analysis, Arithmetic Necessitism.

At type $e$. $W_0$ is extensionally full at its identity: any set of tuples there is the extension of the entity with that value at every map of the constants and $\bot$ at every arrow into the component, pinned down by $\emptyset$ and landing. So the property with the numerals as its extension is an inductive property at $W_0$, the numbers there are the standard numerals, and Sum and Prod are standard on them (a relation refuting a false instance is in the domain). The individuals at $W_0$ are the infinitely many individual constants, so distinct numerals are distinct there and $I^*_e$ holds at $W_0$, and it holds throughout the component by $\Box I^*_e$ at $V_2$; so $\Box I^*_e$ holds at $W_0$. With $A:=\operatorname{Con}(T)$, an arithmetical sentence, $A$ holds at $W_0$, whose arithmetic is standard, and fails at $V_2$; so neither $\Box(I\to A)$ nor $\Box(I\to\neg A)$ holds at $W_0$, and the Necessity of Arithmetic fails. The Necessity of Analysis fails as in coalesced-two-extensional-arithmetics-root, with $W_0$ and $V_2$ for the two components: the Dedekind cuts over the numbers form a real number structure at each, every real number structure at a world is isomorphic to it, and $\exists n\in N\,.\,\operatorname{Proof}_T(n,\bot)$ is false in those at $W_0$ and true in those at $V_2$. Arithmetic Necessitism fails by the inextensibility of $\mathbb N_e$: let $X:=\lambda x\, .\,\mathbb N x\land\neg\exists m\in\mathbb N\, .\,m<x\land \operatorname{Proof}_T(m,\bot)$, a closed pure term. Each number at $W_0$ is a numeral $\mathbf n$, and at every world $X\mathbf n$ holds, since under $I^*_e$ the numbers below $\mathbf n$ are the numerals below it and each $\neg\operatorname{Proof}_T(\mathbf m,\bot)$ is a true bounded sentence, $T$ being consistent. But at $V_2$ some number $m$ codes a proof of $\bot$ from $T$, and $\operatorname{Suc}m$ is a number lacking $X$; so $\mathbb N_e\le X$ fails. The Axiom of Infinity (type e) holds at $W_0$: its finite cardinalities are the numerals, and none holds of the universal property, which has infinitely many instances there.

### Holds Axiom of Infinity (type e). Fails Countable Boolean Completeness.

The root is extensionally full at its identity: any set of tuples there is the extension of the entity with that value at every map of the constants and $\bot$ at every arrow into a component, pinned down by $\emptyset$ and landing; and quantifiers at the identity range over the root domains. So the argument infinitely-many-individuals applies at the root alone: the property of cardinalities true of $Z$ exactly when every property $Z$ holds of has a finite extension contains $\mathbf 0_e$ and is closed under successor, so every finite cardinality has it, and none holds of the universal property, the individuals being the infinitely many individual constants. For the same reason the numbers at $e$ at the root are the numerals. Countable Boolean Completeness fails at type $t$ by the argument parity-family of coalesced-pinned-interpretations-root, which uses only arrows $\langle V,I\rangle$ into one component object and interpretations differing at fresh propositional constants; the property $Y$ is now given the value $\{a_n:n\text{ even}\}$ at every map and $\emptyset$ at the arrows into components, pinned down by $\emptyset$. The family is countable at the root: the relation whose extension at the identity pairs $a_{2k}$ with the numeral $\mathbf k_e$ is in the domain, by extensional fullness at the identity, and is an injection into the numbers at $e$. So the countable property $Y$ has no least upper bound.

### Fails B for pure sentences.

Let $B$ be the sentence of the condition. The worlds seen from a world of a component are worlds of that component, where $B$ is false, so $\Diamond B$ fails at every such world; some such world is seen from the root, where $B$ is true. So the B instance $B\to\Box\Diamond B$ fails at the root.

### Fails Witnessed Possibility, Separated Structure.

Let $B$ be the sentence of the condition, $c$ a relational constant of $\Sigma$, of type $\tau=\bar\sigma t$ (for $\tau=t$ read $\exists\bar y\,.\,x\bar y$ below as $x$), and $P(x):=B\land\exists\bar y\,.\,x\bar y$, a pure formula. At the root $\exists x\,.\,P(x)$ holds, witnessed by $\top_\tau$. But $P(\hat c)$ holds at no arrow out of the root: at an arrow into a component $B$ is false, and at an arrow into the root (the identity, or a map $f$, which carries $\hat c$ to $\widehat{f(c)}$) the entity is a $\hat d$, whose extension at the identity is empty. So $\Diamond P(\hat c)$ fails and Witnessed Possibility fails. ### Holds Independence (signature Σ).

For an individual constant $c$ the instance is vacuous, no term $A\,\bar d$ being of type $e$. Let $c$ be relational, of type $\tau$, and $A$ closed and pure, $\bar d$ constants other than $c$ ($n\ge0$). $A\,\bar{\hat d}$ is pinned down by $\bar d$, the denotation of a closed pure term being pinned down by $\emptyset$. Fix an object $V$ of a component and an interpretation $I$, and let $I^{\prime}$ and $I^{\prime\prime}$ agree with $I$ except that they send $c$ to $\top_\tau$ and to $\bot_\tau$. Then $A\,\bar{\hat d}$ has the same value at $\langle V,I^{\prime}\rangle$ and $\langle V,I^{\prime\prime}\rangle$, while $\hat c$ has there the extensions at $1_V$ of $\top_\tau$ and $\bot_\tau$, which differ, every domain being nonempty. So $\hat c\ne A\,\bar{\hat d}$.

## Notes

A variant of coalesced-pinned-substitutions-root proposed on 29 October 2026 in a trawl for the open questions created by □Atomlessness: the substitution arrows keep the root atomless, and the one component is atomless at every object but disagrees with the root about arithmetic and about BF. On 30 October 2026, at the direction of Cian Dorr, the theory $T$ was strengthened from $\neg$BF (type t) to $\Box\neg$BF (type t), so that BF (type t) is a sentence true at the root alone, and the arguments that merely repeated the argument substitution-root of the group were dropped. Audited by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 29 October 2026; it inherits the model-of-C argument of coalesced-pinned-substitutions-root, which has not had a second reader. No Lean verification is claimed.
