# Fregean Axiom ∧ Infinity (type t) ⇒ False (⊥)

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Classicism (2023 draft); produced by Andrew Bacon and Cian Dorr; recorded by OpenAI Codex (GPT-6), 17 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Fregean Axiom.** Materially equivalent propositions are identical.
- **Infinity (type t).** There are arbitrarily many pairwise distinct entities of this fixed type; one sentence for each positive integer n.

## Conclusion

- **False (⊥).** These premises cannot all hold together.

## Proof

The Fregean Axiom identifies every true proposition with $\top$ and every false one with $\bot$. Its propositional domain therefore has exactly two elements, contradicting the three-distinct-propositions instance of Infinity.
