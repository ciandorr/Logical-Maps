# Full action model: free monoid on countably many generators, glued constants, singleton individuals

This is the variant of the model in the write-up for `full-free-monoid-glued-constants` in which the type-$e$ modalized set is a singleton at every world, with identity counterpart maps. Everything in that write-up goes through unchanged except what depends on the size of the individual domain; this note records the differences.

**Self-similarity.** Properties (E1) and (E2) of §2 there hold trivially: a singleton restricted to any subtree is a singleton, and a product of singletons is a singleton, which is what the book's Definition 18.15 assigns to the root of a coalesced sum of models with singleton individuals. So Lemma 1, the gluing theorem for the frame with singleton individuals, holds as stated, and Lemma 2, Lemma 3 and Theorem 1 follow: Logical Necessity holds at the root.

**Constants.** Corollary 1 fails at type $e$: two individual constants necessarily co-denote, since there is one individual at every world, so $c\ne c'$ for individual constants is not satisfiable over the frame. Logical Necessity itself is unaffected, its instances at individual constants having the form $\Box P[c,c']\leftrightarrow\forall xy\, .\,P$ with both sides evaluated over a one-element domain.

**The pure part.** Everything in §5 holds except at type $e$. The domain at every world has exactly one individual, and the counterpart maps are bijections, so $\forall x^e y^e\, .\,x=y$ is true at every world: the Infinity Schema at type $e$, the Axiom of Infinity at type $e$ and Possible Infinity at type $e$ all fail. The propositional domain is unchanged, so the Axiom of Infinity at type $t$ holds, and the arguments for No Pure Contingency, the necessitations of Atomicity, Actuality, the Strong Leibniz Biconditionals and Rigid Comprehension, Relational Choice and $\Box$BF are as before; at type $e$ every counterpart map is a bijection, so BF there is immediate. ND, B, Possibility (pure) and Fundamental Completeness fail for the reasons given there.
