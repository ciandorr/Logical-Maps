# Mathematical Possibilism ∧ □Rigid Comprehension ∧ Intensional Choice ∧ □Potential Infinity (type e) ⇒ Possible failure of Cardinal CH

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Andrew Bacon (forcing argument), formalized by OpenAI Codex (GPT-6), prompted by Andrew Bacon; recorded by OpenAI Codex (GPT-6), prompted by Andrew Bacon.</p>

## Premises

- **Mathematical Possibilism.** At each admitted carrier type, every complete Boolean algebra whose carrier contains no power-closed subcollection strictly larger than the inexact ordinal benchmark over individuals is relationally order-isomorphic to the propositions entailing some proposition.
- **□Rigid Comprehension.** Necessarily, every relation, including a proposition, is coextensive with a rigid one.
- **Intensional Choice.** If a property is necessarily instantiated, then some property entailing it is necessarily uniquely instantiated.
- **□Potential Infinity (type e).** Necessarily, for every natural number there could be at least that many individuals.

## Conclusion

- **Possible failure of Cardinal CH.** Cardinal CH possibly fails.

## Proof

Use the regular-open completion of finite Cohen conditions at the second Hartogs carrier. The forced family witnesses a subcollection of the rigid power which does not embed into the lower naturals and into which the rigid power does not embed. See the proof in [[power-closed-mp-and-choice-imply-cardinal-ch-contingency]].
