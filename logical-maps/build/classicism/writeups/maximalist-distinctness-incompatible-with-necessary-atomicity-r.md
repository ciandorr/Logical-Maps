# Distinctness (pure) ∧ □Atomicity ⇒ False (⊥)

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Classicism (2023 draft); produced by Andrew Bacon and Cian Dorr; recorded by OpenAI Codex (GPT-6), 17 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Distinctness (pure).** Every closed pure identity not provable in C is false. The non-theorem condition is metalinguistic.
- **□Atomicity.** Every closed instance of Atomicity is necessary. Box the entire object-variable closure; retain the same type range and other schema side conditions.

## Conclusion

- **False (⊥).** These premises cannot all hold together.

## Proof

The cited source construction makes a closed instance of Atomicity false, so its negation is C-consistent. Pure Possibility, equivalent to pure Distinctness, says that negation is possible. The corresponding boxed instance of the second premise says it is impossible.
