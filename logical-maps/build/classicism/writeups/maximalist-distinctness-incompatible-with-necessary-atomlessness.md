# Maximalism (pure) ∧ □Atomlessness ⇒ ⊥

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.maximalist_distinctness_incompatible_with_necessary_atomlessness`; produced by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 29 October 2026, at Cian Dorr's suggestion; recorded by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 29 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Maximalism (pure).** Every closed pure identity not provable in C is false. The non-theorem condition is metalinguistic.
- **□Atomlessness.** Necessarily, every possible proposition has a possible proposition strictly below it.

## Conclusion

- **⊥.** These premises cannot all hold together.

## Proof

Atomlessness is a closed pure sentence, and it fails in full-henkin-singleton-base, a model of C whose propositions are $\top$ and $\bot$, $\top$ being an atom. So its negation is consistent with C, and the possibility form of Maximalism (pure) makes it possible, while □Atomlessness makes Atomlessness necessary.

## Notes

Instance of the pattern of maximalist-distinctness-incompatible-with-necessary-atomicity-t, from the other side: since Atomlessness and Atomicity (type $t$) each hold in some model of C, Maximalism (pure) excludes the necessitation of either.
