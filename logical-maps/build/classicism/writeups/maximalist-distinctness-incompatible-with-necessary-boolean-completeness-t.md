# Maximalism (pure) ∧ □Boolean Completeness (type t) ⇒ ⊥

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.maximalist_distinctness_incompatible_with_necessary_boolean_completeness_t`; produced by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 8 October 2026, at Cian Dorr's suggestion; recorded by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 8 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Maximalism (pure).** Every closed pure identity not provable in C is false. The non-theorem condition is metalinguistic.
- **□Boolean Completeness (type t).** Necessarily, every property of propositions has a greatest lower bound.

## Conclusion

- **⊥.** These premises cannot all hold together.

## Proof

Boolean Completeness (type t) fails in finite-support-permutations, a model of C, so some closed pure instance of it is not a theorem of C and its negation is consistent with C. The possibility form of Maximalism (pure) makes that negation possible, and the boxed principle makes the instance necessary.
