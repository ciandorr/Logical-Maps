# □Actuality ∧ □5-necessities are ND-necessary ⇒ □Inextensible Comprehension

## Premises

- **□Actuality.** Necessarily, there is a true proposition that entails every true proposition.
- **□5-necessities are ND-necessary.** Necessarily, whatever is 5-necessary is ND-necessary. In C this is the identity $\Box_5=\Box_{\ne}$.

## Conclusion

- **□Inextensible Comprehension.** Necessarily, every relation, including a proposition, is coextensive with an inextensible one.
