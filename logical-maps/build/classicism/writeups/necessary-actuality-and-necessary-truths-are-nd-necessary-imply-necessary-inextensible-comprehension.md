# □Actuality ∧ □Truths are ND-necessary ⇒ □Inextensible Comprehension

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.necessary_actuality_and_necessary_truths_are_nd_necessary_imply_necessary_inextensible_comprehension`; produced by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 25 September 2026 (unboxed form); boxed form recorded by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), 19 October 2026; recorded by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 19 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **□Actuality.** Necessarily, there is a true proposition that entails every true proposition.
- **□Truths are ND-necessary.** Necessarily, every truth is ND-necessary. In C this is the identity $\Box_{\ne}=\lambda p\, .\,p$.

## Conclusion

- **□Inextensible Comprehension.** Necessarily, every relation, including a proposition, is coextensive with an inextensible one.

## Proof

The boxed form of [[actuality-and-truths-are-nd-necessary-imply-inextensible-comprehension]]. Its proof derives each instance of the conclusion in C from finitely many instances of the premises; necessitate the conditional and apply K.
