# □Atomicity (type t) ∧ □Atoms are strong worlds (type t) ⇒ □Strong Leibniz Biconditionals (type t)

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 25 October 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 25 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **□Atomicity (type t).** Necessarily, every non-bottom proposition has an atom below it.
- **□Atoms are strong worlds (type t).** Necessarily, every atom is a strong world proposition.

## Conclusion

- **□Strong Leibniz Biconditionals (type t).** Necessarily, every possible proposition is entailed by a strong world proposition, one that is possible and necessarily entails every proposition or its negation.

## Proof

Necessitate the recorded [[atomicity-t-and-atoms-are-strong-worlds-t-imply-strong-leibniz-t]], a theorem of C between closed instances of its principles, and distribute by K.
