# □Atoms are self-atoms ∧ □Self-atoms are strong worlds ⇒ □Atoms are strong worlds

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 25 October 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 25 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **□Atoms are self-atoms.** Necessarily, at each relational type, every atom is a self-atom: necessarily, if it is instantiated, it is an atom.
- **□Self-atoms are strong worlds.** Necessarily, at each relational type, every self-atom is a strong world: it necessarily entails every entity of the type or its negation.

## Conclusion

- **□Atoms are strong worlds.** Necessarily, at each relational type, every atom is a strong world: it necessarily entails every entity of the type or its negation.
