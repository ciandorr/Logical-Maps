# □Axiom of Infinity (type e) ⇒ □Potential Infinity (type e)

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.necessary_axiom_of_infinity_e_implies_necessary_potential_infinity_e`; produced by Claude Fable 5.2 (Anthropic), prompted by Zachary Goodsell, 24 November 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.2 (Anthropic), prompted by Zachary Goodsell, 24 November 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **□Axiom of Infinity (type e).** Necessarily, there are not finitely many individuals.

## Conclusion

- **□Potential Infinity (type e).** Necessarily, for every natural number there could be at least that many individuals.

## Proof

The derivation of Potential Infinity from the Axiom of Infinity at type $e$ ([[axiom-of-infinity-e-implies-potential-infinity-e]]) is in C, so its conclusion follows necessarily from its premise: necessitate it and apply K.

## Notes

With the Peano form of the conclusion this is the Background lemma [[necessary-infinity-injective-successor]], that the boxed Axiom of Infinity makes successor necessarily injective on the numbers.
