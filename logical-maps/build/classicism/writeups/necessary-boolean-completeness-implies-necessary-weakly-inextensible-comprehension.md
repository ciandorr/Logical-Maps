# □Boolean Completeness ⇒ □Weakly Inextensible Comprehension

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.necessary_boolean_completeness_implies_necessary_weakly_inextensible_comprehension`; produced by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 25 September 2026 (unboxed form); boxed form recorded by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), 19 October 2026; recorded by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 19 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **□Boolean Completeness.** Necessarily, every property of entities of a relational type has a greatest lower bound in that type.

## Conclusion

- **□Weakly Inextensible Comprehension.** Necessarily, every relation, including a proposition, is coextensive with a weakly inextensible one.
