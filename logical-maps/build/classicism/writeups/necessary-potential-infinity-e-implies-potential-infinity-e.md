# □Potential Infinity (type e) ⇒ Potential Infinity (type e)

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.necessary_potential_infinity_e_implies_potential_infinity_e`; produced by Claude Fable 5.2 (Anthropic), prompted by Zachary Goodsell, 24 November 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.2 (Anthropic), prompted by Zachary Goodsell, 24 November 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **□Potential Infinity (type e).** Necessarily, for every natural number there could be at least that many individuals.

## Conclusion

- **Potential Infinity (type e).** For every natural number, there could be at least that many individuals: each number counted at type $e$ possibly holds of some property of individuals.

## Proof

T: $\Box\phi\to\phi$.

## Notes

The converse holds given B for pure sentences ([[potential-infinity-e-and-pure-b-imply-necessary-potential-infinity-e]]) and fails in C: a world from which the individuals could be ever more numerous may see one from which they could not, as at the roots of the coalesced sums.
