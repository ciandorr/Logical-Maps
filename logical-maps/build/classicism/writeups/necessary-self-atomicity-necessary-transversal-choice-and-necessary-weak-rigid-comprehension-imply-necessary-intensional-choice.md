# □Self-Atomicity ∧ □Transversal Choice ∧ □Weak Rigid Comprehension ⇒ □Intensional Choice

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 30 October 2026, from the unboxed result; recorded by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 30 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **□Self-Atomicity.** Necessarily, at each relational type, every possible entity (one distinct from bottom) is entailed by a self-atom: a possible entity that necessarily, if instantiated, is an atom.
- **□Transversal Choice.** Necessarily, every equivalence relation, at any type including e, has a transversal, that is, a property with exactly one instance in each of its cells.
- **□Weak Rigid Comprehension.** Necessarily, every relation, including a proposition, is coextensive with a weakly rigid one.

## Conclusion

- **□Intensional Choice.** Necessarily, if a property is necessarily instantiated, then some property entailing it is necessarily uniquely instantiated.

The proofs of [[self-atomicity-zorn-and-weak-rigid-comprehension-imply-intensional-choice]] and of [[transversal-choice-r-implies-zorn-r]] use their premises only at finitely many closed instances and nothing beyond C. Necessitate that theorem and apply K with the boxed premise instances. Generalization over the admitted types is metalinguistic; no type quantifier is placed inside the box.
