# □Strong Atomicity (type t) ⇒ □Self-Atomicity (type t)

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 25 October 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 25 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **□Strong Atomicity (type t).** Necessarily, every possible proposition is entailed by a strong atom, one that is possible and necessarily entails every proposition or its negation.

## Conclusion

- **□Self-Atomicity (type t).** Necessarily, every possible proposition is entailed by a self-atom: a possible proposition that necessarily, if true, is an atom, that is, one that entails that it is the actual world.
