# □Strong Atomicity (type t) ⇒ □Strong Actuality

## Premises

- **□Strong Atomicity (type t).** Necessarily, every possible proposition is entailed by a strong atom, one that is possible and necessarily entails every proposition or its negation.

## Conclusion

- **□Strong Actuality.** Necessarily, there is a true strong atom: a true proposition that necessarily entails every proposition or its negation.

## Proof

The implication from Strong Atomicity (type $t$) to Strong Actuality is a theorem of C between closed sentences (the type-$t$ principle has no type parameters); necessitate it and distribute by K.
