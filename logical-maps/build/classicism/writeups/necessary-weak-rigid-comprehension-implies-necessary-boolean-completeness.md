# □Weak Rigid Comprehension ⇒ □Boolean Completeness

## Premises

- **□Weak Rigid Comprehension.** Necessarily, every relation, including a proposition, is coextensive with a weakly rigid one.

## Conclusion

- **□Boolean Completeness.** Necessarily, every property of entities of a relational type has a greatest lower bound in that type.
