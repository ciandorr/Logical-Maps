# No Pure Contingency ∧ Atomlessness ⇒ □Atomlessness

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.no_pure_contingency_and_atomlessness_imply_necessary_atomlessness`; produced by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 29 October 2026; recorded by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 29 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **No Pure Contingency.** Each closed sentence of the pure language, if true, is necessary. This is a sentence schema, not a quantifier over propositions.
- **Atomlessness.** Atomlessness in the displayed closed propositional formulation.

## Conclusion

- **□Atomlessness.** Necessarily, every possible proposition has a possible proposition strictly below it.

## Proof

Atomlessness is a closed sentence of the pure language, stated with object-language quantifiers and no nonlogical constant. Given Atomlessness it is true, and No Pure Contingency makes a true closed pure sentence necessary.

## Notes

One of the uniform family by which No Pure Contingency carries a pure principle to its boxed form.
