# No Pure Contingency ∧ Atoms are strong worlds ⇒ □Atoms are strong worlds

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 25 October 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 25 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **No Pure Contingency.** Each closed sentence of the pure language, if true, is necessary. This is a sentence schema, not a quantifier over propositions.
- **Atoms are strong worlds.** At each relational type, every atom is a strong world: it necessarily entails every entity of the type or its negation.

## Conclusion

- **□Atoms are strong worlds.** Necessarily, at each relational type, every atom is a strong world: it necessarily entails every entity of the type or its negation.

## Proof

Every closed instance of Atoms are strong worlds is a closed sentence of the pure language, stated with object-language quantifiers and no nonlogical constant. Each is true, and No Pure Contingency makes each necessary, which is □Atoms are strong worlds.
