# No Pure Contingency ∧ Self-atoms are strong atoms ⇒ □Self-atoms are strong atoms

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 25 October 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 25 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **No Pure Contingency.** Each closed sentence of the pure language, if true, is necessary. This is a sentence schema, not a quantifier over propositions.
- **Self-atoms are strong atoms.** At each relational type, every self-atom is a strong atom: it necessarily entails every entity of the type or its negation.

## Conclusion

- **□Self-atoms are strong atoms.** Necessarily, at each relational type, every self-atom is a strong atom: it necessarily entails every entity of the type or its negation.

## Proof

Every closed instance of Self-atoms are strong atoms is a closed sentence of the pure language, stated with object-language quantifiers and no nonlogical constant. Each is true, and No Pure Contingency makes each necessary, which is □Self-atoms are strong atoms.
