# Maximalism (pure) ∧ □Atoms are self-atoms (type t) ⇒ ⊥

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 25 October 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 25 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Maximalism (pure).** Every closed pure identity not provable in C is false. The non-theorem condition is metalinguistic.
- **□Atoms are self-atoms (type t).** Necessarily, every atom is a self-atom: necessarily, if it is true, it is an atom.

## Conclusion

- **⊥.** These premises cannot all hold together.

## Proof

Atoms are self-atoms (type t) is a closed pure sentence. It fails at the evaluation point of the recorded [[full-idempotent-monoid]], an action model and so a model of C by Theorem 3.23, where the atom $\{k\}$ becomes $\{1,k\}$ under $k$. Possibility Maximalism (pure) therefore makes its negation possible, while □Atoms are self-atoms (type t) says the sentence is necessary.
