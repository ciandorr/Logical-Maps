# Maximalism (pure) ∧ □Potential Infinity (type e) ⇒ ⊥

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.possibility_and_necessary_potential_infinity_e_incompatible`; produced by Claude Fable 5.2 (Anthropic), prompted by Zachary Goodsell, 24 November 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.2 (Anthropic), prompted by Zachary Goodsell, 24 November 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Maximalism (pure).** Every closed pure identity not provable in C is false. The non-theorem condition is metalinguistic.
- **□Potential Infinity (type e).** Necessarily, for every natural number there could be at least that many individuals.

## Conclusion

- **⊥.** These premises cannot all hold together.

## Proof

Potential Infinity (type $e$) is a closed pure sentence, and its negation is consistent with C: in the full Henkin model on one individual every type is finite, so the Peano form $I^*_e$ fails there (the numerals cannot be pairwise distinct), and with it Potential Infinity (Background, [[potential-infinity-forms]]). So Possibility Maximalism (pure), which follows from Distinctness Maximalism (pure), gives $\Diamond\neg\operatorname{PotInf}_e$, against $\Box\operatorname{PotInf}_e$.

## Notes

An instance of the pattern of [[possibility-and-necessary-barcan-t-incompatible]]: Possibility Maximalism (pure) refutes $\Box Y$ whenever a model of C refutes the closed pure $Y$. Since the boxed Axiom of Infinity at type $e$ implies □Potential Infinity there, Maximalism refutes that too. The unboxed Potential Infinity (type $e$) follows from Maximalism, through Possible Infinity.
