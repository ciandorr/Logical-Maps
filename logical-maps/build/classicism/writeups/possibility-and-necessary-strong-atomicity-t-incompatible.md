# Maximalism (pure) ∧ □Strong Atomicity (type t) ⇒ ⊥

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Cian Dorr, observation of 23 September 2026 (whatever is consistent with Maximalist Classicism is made possible by Strong Possibility); proof written out by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 23 September 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 23 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Maximalism (pure).** Every closed pure identity not provable in C is false. The non-theorem condition is metalinguistic.
- **□Strong Atomicity (type t).** Necessarily, every possible proposition is entailed by a strong atom, one that is possible and necessarily entails every proposition or its negation.

## Conclusion

- **⊥.** These premises cannot all hold together.

## Proof

Strong Atomicity at type $t$ forms a closed pure sentence. It fails at the root of the recorded coalesced sums (for instance coalesced-maximalist-root), where Atomicity (type t) fails and Strong Atomicity (type t) entail it; these are action models, so models of C by Theorem 3.23. Possibility Maximalism (pure) therefore makes the negation possible, while $\Box$Strong Atomicity (type t) says the sentence is necessary.

## Notes

Instance of a general pattern: Possibility Maximalism (pure) yields $\Diamond\neg Y$ for every closed pure $Y$ whose negation is consistent with C, so it refutes $\Box Y$ whenever a model of C refutes $Y$. For the other boxed principles on the map the incompatibility with Possibility Maximalism (pure) is already derivable, through the recorded equivalence of Possibility Maximalism (pure) with Distinctness Maximalism (pure) and the Distinctness Maximalism incompatibilities; this record covers a case that was not. Both Strong Possibility principles inherit it, the pure one through Possibility Maximalism (pure) and the Σ one through Possibility Maximalism (signature Σ). Cian Dorr observed on 23 September 2026 that anything consistent with Maximalist Classicism is made possible by Strong Possibility; since it is already made possible by Possibility Maximalism itself, the arrows are recorded here, at the weaker premise.
