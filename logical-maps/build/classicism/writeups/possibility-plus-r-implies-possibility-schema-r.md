# Possibility+ ⇒ Possibility (pure)

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Classicism (2023 draft); produced by Andrew Bacon and Cian Dorr; recorded by OpenAI Codex (GPT-6), 17 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Possibility+.** The strengthened Possibility schema for pure C-consistent formulas whose only free variables are the displayed individual variables; include the empty tuple.

## Conclusion

- **Possibility (pure).** Every closed pure sentence consistent with C is possible.

## Proof

Take the empty tuple of individual variables. Its distinctness condition is vacuous; the instances are pure Possibility.
