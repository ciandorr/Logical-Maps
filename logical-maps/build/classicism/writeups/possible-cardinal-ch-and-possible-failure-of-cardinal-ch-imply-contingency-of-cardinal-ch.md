# Possible Cardinal CH ∧ Possible failure of Cardinal CH ⇒ Contingency of Cardinal CH

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.possible_cardinal_ch_and_possible_failure_of_cardinal_ch_imply_contingency_of_cardinal_ch`; produced by Claude Fable 5.2 (Anthropic), prompted by Zachary Goodsell, 25 November 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.2 (Anthropic), prompted by Zachary Goodsell, 25 November 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Possible Cardinal CH.** Cardinal CH possibly holds.
- **Possible failure of Cardinal CH.** Cardinal CH possibly fails.

## Conclusion

- **Contingency of Cardinal CH.** Cardinal CH possibly holds and possibly fails.

## Proof

Contingency of Cardinal CH is the conjunction of the two premises: $\Diamond C_N\land\Diamond\neg C_N$, with the same $N$ and $S$.
