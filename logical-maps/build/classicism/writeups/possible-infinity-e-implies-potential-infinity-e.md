# Possible Infinity (type e) ⇒ Potential Infinity (type e)

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.possible_infinity_e_implies_potential_infinity_e`; produced by Claude Fable 5.2 (Anthropic), prompted by Zachary Goodsell, 24 November 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.2 (Anthropic), prompted by Zachary Goodsell, 24 November 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Possible Infinity (type e).** Possibly there are not finitely many individuals: the Axiom of Infinity at this type, under a diamond.

## Conclusion

- **Potential Infinity (type e).** For every natural number, there could be at least that many individuals: each number counted at type $e$ possibly holds of some property of individuals.

## Proof

The Axiom of Infinity at type $e$ implies Potential Infinity there, in C ([[axiom-of-infinity-e-implies-potential-infinity-e]]); necessitating that derivation, Possible Infinity gives $\Diamond\operatorname{PotInf}_e$. And Potential Infinity is true if possible (Background, [[potential-infinity-forms]], part 2): if a number $n$ had $\neg\Diamond\exists X\, .\,nX$, then necessarily $n$ would be a number, numbers being necessarily numbers, and necessarily $\neg\Diamond\exists X\, .\,nX$, by 4, so that Potential Infinity would be impossible.

## Notes

So Potential Infinity (type $e$) is the weakest of the infinity principles at this type that the map records. The converse fails: in [[full-omega-chain-new-individuals]] every world has finitely many individuals and sees worlds with more.
