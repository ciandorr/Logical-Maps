# Relational Choice ∧ Boolean Completeness ⇒ Transversal Choice

<p class='cert'>Conjecture — Source: Misc.; produced by Zachary Goodsell, conjecture of 27 September 2026; recorded by Codex (OpenAI), 27 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Relational Choice.** Every serial binary relation has a functional subrelation.
- **Boolean Completeness.** Every property of entities of a relational type has a greatest lower bound in that type.

## Conclusion

- **Transversal Choice.** Every equivalence relation, at any type including e, has a transversal, that is, a property with exactly one instance in each of its cells.

## Notes

Does adding Boolean Completeness to Relational Choice suffice for Transversal Choice in C? The question without Boolean Completeness is recorded separately. No proof was supplied. A proof of this implication, or a model of C satisfying both premises and failing Transversal Choice, would settle this conjecture.
