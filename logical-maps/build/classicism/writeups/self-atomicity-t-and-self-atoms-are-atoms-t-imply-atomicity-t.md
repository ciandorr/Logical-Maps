# Self-Atomicity (type t) ∧ Self-atoms are atoms (type t) ⇒ Atomicity (type t)

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 25 October 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 25 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Self-Atomicity (type t).** Every possible proposition is entailed by a self-atom: a possible proposition that necessarily, if true, is an atom, that is, one that entails that it is the actual world.
- **Self-atoms are atoms (type t).** Every self-atom is an atom.

## Conclusion

- **Atomicity (type t).** Every non-bottom proposition has an atom below it.

## Proof

A possible entity has a self-atom below it, which is an atom.

## Notes

Atomicity does not imply Self-Atomicity: the full idempotent monoid has the atom $\{k\}$, which under $k$ becomes $\{1,k\}$.
