# Self-atoms are strong atoms (type t) ⇒ Self-atoms are atoms (type t)

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 25 October 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 25 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Self-atoms are strong atoms (type t).** Every self-atom is a strong atom.

## Conclusion

- **Self-atoms are atoms (type t).** Every self-atom is an atom.

## Proof

A strong atom is an atom: T on its boxed clause gives that it lies below every proposition or its negation, and it is not bottom (the argument recorded for [[strong-atomicity-t-implies-atomicity-t]]).
