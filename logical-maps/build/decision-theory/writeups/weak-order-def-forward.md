# Completeness ∧ Transitivity ⇒ Weak order

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by definition.</p>

## Premises

- **Completeness.** For all p, q ∈ Δ(X): p ≽ q or q ≽ p.
- **Transitivity.** For all p, q, r ∈ Δ(X): if p ≽ q and q ≽ r then p ≽ r.

## Conclusion

- **Weak order.** ≽ is complete and transitive.

## Proof

By definition of weak order.
