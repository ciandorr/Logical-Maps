# Weak order ⇒ Transitivity

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by definition.</p>

## Premises

- **Weak order.** $\succeq$ is complete and transitive.

## Conclusion

- **Transitivity.** For all $p, q, r \in \Delta (X)$: if $p \succeq q$ and $q \succeq r$ then $p \succeq r$.

## Proof

By definition of weak order.
