## Premises

- **□$\Diamond_\vee$ entails Truth.** $\Box (\forall p:t$. $\Diamond_\vee$$p \to p$)

## Conclusion

- **$\Diamond_\vee$ = Truth.** $\Diamond_\vee$ $= (\lambda p:t. p)$
