# A PSb–PSc–PSd operator exists ⇒ A PSb–PSd operator exists

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Codex (GPT-6), 13 September 2026; recorded by Codex (GPT-6), manuscript transcription.</p>

## Premises

- **A PSb–PSc–PSd operator exists.** $\exists f:tt. \operatorname{PSb}(f) \land \operatorname{PSc}(f) \land \operatorname{PSd}(f)$

## Conclusion

- **A PSb–PSd operator exists.** $\exists f:tt. \operatorname{PSb}(f) \land \operatorname{PSd}(f)$

## Proof

Eliminate the existential, retain its PSb and PSd conjuncts, and reuse the same witness.

## Notes

Informal proof; no independent checker or Lean verification.
