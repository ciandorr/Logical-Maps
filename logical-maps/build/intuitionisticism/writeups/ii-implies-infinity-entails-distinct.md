## Conclusion

- **$\Diamond_\infty$ entails ≠⊥.** $\forall p:t$. $\Diamond_\infty$$p \to (p \ne \bot )$

## Proof

Given $\Diamond_\infty$p, choose its candidate witness P with $\mathrm{Possibility}_\infty$(P) and Pp. The candidate predicate implies $\operatorname{PSb}(P)$, namely $\neg P\bot$. If $p=\bot$, substitution in Pp gives $P\bot , a$ contradiction. Thus $p\ne \bot$. Discharge and generalize p. This bound only needs PSb and does not assume the disputed infinitary-to-binary reduction.
