## Conclusion

- **Nonfalsity candidate characterization.** $\mathrm{Possibility}_\vee$(¬ ∘ ¬) = □(∀p:t. ¬p ∨ ¬¬p)

## Proof

For P=¬∘¬, PSa follows from □(p→q)→(p→q) and intuitionistic monotonicity of double negation; PSb is also an intuitionistic theorem. Universally close and necessitate both. Thus $\mathrm{Possibility}_\vee$(¬∘¬) is equivalent to necessary nonfalsity distribution. Theorem 8’s case argument gives the assumption-free equivalence of that sentence with necessary weak excluded middle (the two implications are recorded separately). Apply standing propositional intensionality to this assumption-free equivalence to obtain the displayed identity.
