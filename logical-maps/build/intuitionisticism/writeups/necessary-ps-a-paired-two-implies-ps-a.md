# □PSa (□₂, ◇₂) ⇒ PSa (□₂, ◇₂)

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Possibility in Intuitionistic Higher-Order Logic; produced by Zachary Goodsell; proof transcribed and expanded from the manuscript; recorded by Codex (GPT-6), manuscript transcription.</p>

## Premises

- **□PSa (□₂, ◇₂).** $\Box (\forall p,q:t. \Box _{2}(p \to q) \to (\Diamond _{2}p \to \Diamond _{2}q))$

## Conclusion

- **PSa (□₂, ◇₂).** $\forall p,q:t. \Box _{2}(p \to q) \to (\Diamond _{2}p \to \Diamond _{2}q)$

## Proof

Apply $\Box A\to A$ to the entire paired modal condition. The inner $\Box _{2}$ is unchanged.

## Notes

Informal proof; no independent checker or Lean verification.
