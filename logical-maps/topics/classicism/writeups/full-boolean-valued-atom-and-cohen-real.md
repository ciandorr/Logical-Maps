# Full Boolean-valued model: one atom and the Cohen algebra

Underlying construction by Andrew Bacon (*Could the truths of mathematics have been
different?*, online appendix §B.2). The choice of the Cohen algebra over $L$, and the
verification of the additional premises, are by GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI), prompted by Daniel Kodsi,
in a research note of 25 October 2026. Recorded by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian
Dorr, 28 October 2026.

This is the full Boolean-valued model with $B=\mathbf 2\times A$, where
$A=\operatorname{RO}(\operatorname{Add}(\omega,1))$ adds one Cohen real. The individuals are
$\omega$, and the model is evaluated at the atom $a=(1,0_A)$. It differs from
full-boolean-valued-atom-and-atomless in two respects:

- that model's algebra is chosen by cases on CH, so as to make CH contingent, whereas this
  algebra leaves CH unchanged;
- this model has infinitely many individuals.

## Non-contingency of CH

Write $\pi$ and $\rho$ for the two coordinate projections, which are complete homomorphisms. By
extensional fullness, a box-free sentence about real number structures has:

- as its $\pi$-coordinate, its truth in the full standard model over $V$;
- as its $\rho$-coordinate, its value in $V^A$, with relations on a ground set ranging over all
  names for subsets of it.

**Real number structures exist in both coordinates.** Each coordinate has a real number
structure on a subset of $D_t=B$, because $|B|=2^{\aleph_0}$ both in $V$ and in $V^A$. And real
number structures are unique up to isomorphism in each coordinate.

**CH has the same truth value in both.** Forcing with $A$ is ccc, so it preserves cardinals. It
adds no more than $(2^{\aleph_0})^{\aleph_0}=2^{\aleph_0}$ nice names for reals, so
$(2^{\aleph_0})^{V^A}=(2^{\aleph_0})^V$.

So, at every type $\sigma$, whichever of

$$
\forall\mathfrak R\,.\,\operatorname{Real}_\sigma(\mathfrak R)\to\operatorname{CH}(\mathfrak R)
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\forall\mathfrak R\,.\,\operatorname{Real}_\sigma(\mathfrak R)\to\neg\operatorname{CH}(\mathfrak R)
$$

is true in $V$ has value $(1,1_A)=\top$. Its box is then $\top$, and Non-contingency of CH holds.

## Necessity of Analysis, built in $L$

In the variant built in $L$, generated by the group, the group's argument
necessity-of-analysis-constructible applies. Let $\Phi$ be the $\Pi^1_3$ analytical sentence
saying that every real is constructible.

- $\Phi$ holds in $L$.
- Forcing with $A$ adds a Cohen real over $L$, which is not constructible.

So $\Phi$ has value $(1,0_A)$ and $\neg\Phi$ has value $(0,1_A)$, and both disjuncts of that
instance of Necessity of Analysis are $\bot$. Astra's note writes $\Phi$ out in the first-order
language of an ordered field with a natural-numbers predicate, coding sets of naturals by
base-4 expansions. CH holds in $L$ and in $L^A$, so the variant combines the failure of
Necessity of Analysis with Non-contingency of CH.

## The other verdicts

The arguments for the other verdicts are those of full-boolean-valued-atom-and-atomless, whose
proofs use only the following:

- fullness;
- crisp identity;
- a well-ordering of each domain;
- that $A$ is atomless and adds a Cohen real;
- that $B$ has at least continuum many elements.

All of these hold here. The infinitely many individuals add the Axiom of Infinity at $e$.

Astra's note also verifies directly a number of verdicts that the map derives:

- □Relational Choice, by disjointifying along a well-order;
- unboxed Rigid Comprehension, by crisp characteristic functions;
- unboxed Boolean Completeness;
- the failures of □Functional Choice and □Rigid Comprehension.
