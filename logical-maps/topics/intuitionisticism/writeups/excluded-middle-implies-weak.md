# LEM ⇒ WLEM

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Codex (GPT-6), 13 September 2026; recorded by Codex (GPT-6), manuscript transcription.</p>

## Premises

- **LEM.** Every proposition is true or has an intuitionistic negation that is true.

## Conclusion

- **WLEM.** $\forall p:t. \neg p \lor \neg \neg p$

## Proof

Fix p and use the selected excluded-middle sentence at p. Its $\neg p$ case gives the left disjunct; its p case gives $\neg \neg p$ and the right disjunct. Generalize p.

Informal proof; no independent checker or Lean verification.
