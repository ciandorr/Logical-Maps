# ⊤ ⇒ 4 (□)

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: The Broadest Necessity; produced by Andrew Bacon; intuitionistic proof as presented by Zachary Goodsell; recorded by Codex (GPT-6), manuscript transcription.</p>

## Premises

- ⊤

## Conclusion

- **4 (□).** ∀p:t. □p → □□p

## Proof

If p = ⊤, Leibniz substitution gives (p = ⊤) = (⊤ = ⊤). The reflexivity theorem ⊤ = ⊤ is identical to ⊤ by assumption-free propositional intensionality. Transitivity gives (p = ⊤) = ⊤. Discharge and generalize.
