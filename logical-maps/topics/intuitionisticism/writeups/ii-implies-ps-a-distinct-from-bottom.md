# ⊤ ⇒ PSa (≠⊥)

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Codex (GPT-6), 13 September 2026; recorded by Codex (GPT-6), manuscript transcription.</p>

## Premises

- ⊤

## Conclusion

- **PSa (≠⊥).** For every p and q, □(p → q) implies that p ≠ ⊥ implies q ≠ ⊥, with □p defined as p = ⊤.

## Proof

Given □(p→q) and p≠⊥, suppose q=⊥. Substitution turns the boxed implication into □¬p, hence p=⊥ by footnote 5, a contradiction. Thus q≠⊥. Discharge and generalize p,q.
