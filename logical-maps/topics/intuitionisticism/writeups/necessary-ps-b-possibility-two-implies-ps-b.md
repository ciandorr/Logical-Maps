## Premises

- **□PSb (◇₂).** □(¬◇₂(⊥))

## Conclusion

- **PSb (◇₂).** ◇₂ does not hold of the contradiction ⊥.

## Proof

Apply the standing theorem □A → A to the entire quantified PS condition. This is modal elimination, with no necessitation of assumptions.
