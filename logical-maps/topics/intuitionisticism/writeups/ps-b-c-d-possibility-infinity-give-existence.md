# PSb ($\Diamond_\infty$) ∧ PSc ($\Diamond_\infty$) ∧ PSd ($\Diamond_\infty$) ⇒ A PSb–PSc–PSd operator exists

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Codex (GPT-6), 13 September 2026; recorded by Codex (GPT-6), manuscript transcription.</p>

## Premises

- **PSb ($\Diamond_\infty$).** $\Diamond_\infty$ does not hold of the contradiction ⊥.
- **PSc ($\Diamond_\infty$).** For every p and q, $\Diamond_\infty$(p ∨ q) implies $\Diamond_\infty$(p) ∨ $\Diamond_\infty$(q).
- **PSd ($\Diamond_\infty$).** For every p and q, if $\Diamond_\infty$(p) implies □q, then □(p → q), with □p defined as p = ⊤.

## Conclusion

- **A PSb–PSc–PSd operator exists.** ∃f:tt. PSb(f) ∧ PSc(f) ∧ PSd(f)

## Proof

Use the defined operator $\Diamond_\infty$ as the witness f, preserving all three selected conjuncts.

## Notes

Informal proof; no independent checker or Lean verification.
