# PSb ($\Diamond_\vee$) ∧ PSd ($\Diamond_\vee$) ⇒ A PSb–PSd operator exists

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Codex (GPT-6), 13 September 2026; recorded by Codex (GPT-6), manuscript transcription.</p>

## Premises

- **PSb ($\Diamond_\vee$).** $\Diamond_\vee$ does not hold of the contradiction ⊥.
- **PSd ($\Diamond_\vee$).** For every p and q, if $\Diamond_\vee$(p) implies □q, then □(p → q), with □p defined as p = ⊤.

## Conclusion

- **A PSb–PSd operator exists.** ∃f:tt. PSb(f) ∧ PSd(f)

## Proof

Use the defined operator $\Diamond_\vee$ as the witness f in the existential formula.

Informal proof; no independent checker or Lean verification.
