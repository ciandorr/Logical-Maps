- **□PSc (≠⊥).** $\Box (\forall p, q : t. (((p \lor q) \ne \bot ) \to ((p \ne \bot ) \lor (q \ne \bot ))))$

## Conclusion

- **PSc (≠⊥).** For every p and $q, (p \lor q) \ne \bot$ implies $p \ne \bot$ or $q \ne \bot$.
