# Positive Affine Invariance implies Rational Affine Preservation

scope of the two principles. Positive Affine Invariance gives

$$X\succeq Y\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad aX+b\succeq aY+b.$$

This directly preserves weak preference. If $X\succ Y$, apply the
biconditional also to the reversed pair: $aX+b\succeq aY+b$ holds and
$aY+b\succeq aX+b$ does not. Thus $aX+b\succ aY+b$, as required.
