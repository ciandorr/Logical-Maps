Construction and the DTU axioms

Take O=ℝO=\mathbb R with its usual order, Borel structure and identity utility, and all measurable real random variables on the topic’s fixed probability space. Put

qt(x)=max(−t,min(x,2t)),vX(t)=E[qt(X)],t>0.q_t(x)=\max(-t,\min(x,2t)),\qquad v_X(t)=E[q_t(X)],\qquad t>0.

Fix an ultrafilter 𝒰\mathcal U on (0,∞)(0,\infty) containing every tail (s,∞)(s,\infty). Define

$$X\succeq Y\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \forall\varepsilon>0,\quad \{t:v_X(t)-v_Y(t)\ge-\varepsilon\}\in\mathcal U.\tag{1}$$

Existence of this ultrafilter uses the usual ultrafilter lemma, as in the source construction; this is a mathematical existence model, not an implemented algorithm for arbitrary comparisons.

Reflexivity is immediate. For transitivity, intersect the two large sets obtained with ε/2\varepsilon/2. If $X\not\succeq Y$, there is an ε>0\varepsilon>0 such that vY−vX>εv_Y-v_X>\varepsilon on a large set. Consequently Y≽XY\succeq X, proving Totality.

Equal laws have equal vv-functions, so Stochastic Equivalence holds. Constants and all values of each simple gamble eventually lie inside the clipping window. Their comparisons are therefore exactly their ordinary expectation comparisons. The normalized chart is the identity: the binary calibration equations give the usual real values. This proves Rich Outcomes and Simple Expected Utility, including all standing chart conventions.

For the fixed randomized mixture, equality of its law with the mixture law gives

vMp(X,Z)=pvX+(1−p)vZ.v_{M_p(X,Z)}=p v_X+(1-p)v_Z.

Subtracting the corresponding expression with YY and rescaling ε\varepsilon by p>0p>0 proves the full Mixture Independence biconditional.

Write SX(s)=Pr(X>s)S_X(s)=\Pr(X>s). The elementary clipped-tail identity gives

$$v_X(t)-v_Y(t)=\int_{-t}^{2t}(S_X(s)-S_Y(s))\,ds.\tag{2}$$

If SX≥SYS_X\ge S_Y pointwise, this is nonnegative. If an inequality is strict at a threshold, right continuity supplies an interval where the difference is bounded below by a positive constant. All sufficiently large windows contain that interval, giving a fixed strictly positive gap in (2). Thus (1) preserves both weak and strict Stochastic Dominance. These verifications establish the entire DTU package.

Two useful consequences of (1) will be used below. Adding a function tending to zero to vX−vYv_X-v_Y does not change the comparison, by an ε/2\varepsilon/2 argument. If vX−vYv_X-v_Y tends to a finite number rr, the comparison holds exactly when r≥0r\ge0; when the limit is zero both directions hold. A limit of +∞+\infty or −∞-\infty yields the corresponding strict comparison.

Expectation, CDF area, and continuity

For integrable XX, qt(X)→Xq_t(X)\to X pointwise and |qt(X)|≤|X||q_t(X)|\le |X|. Dominated convergence gives vX(t)→E[X]v_X(t)\to E[X]. The preceding limit criterion proves Expected Utility.

Clipping is 1-Lipschitz. Whenever E|X−Y|<∞E|X-Y|<\infty,

qt(X)−qt(Y)→X−Y,|qt(X)−qt(Y)|≤|X−Y|.q_t(X)-q_t(Y)\longrightarrow X-Y,\qquad |q_t(X)-q_t(Y)|\le |X-Y|.

Hence vX−vY→E[X−Y]v_X-v_Y\to E[X-Y], even when the individual expectations do not exist. The limit criterion proves Relative Expectation for the variables’ actual coupling, without replacing that coupling by a more convenient one.

For CDF-Area Extension, put d=SX−SYd=S_X-S_Y and A±=∫ℝd±A_\pm=\int_{\mathbb R}d_\pm. The nested windows [−t,2t][-t,2t] exhaust ℝ\mathbb R, so their positive and negative integrals tend monotonically to A+A_+ and A−A_-. If at least one area is finite, (2) tends to their well-defined difference in the extended reals. If both are finite and equal, the limit is zero; if the positive area is larger, including the case of a single positive infinity, the limit is positive; the reverse cases are negative. Thus X≽YX\succeq Y holds exactly when A+≥A−A_+\ge A_- in every case specified by the principle. Nothing here specifies the both-infinite pairs.

For L¹ Continuity, suppose δn=E|Xn−X|→0\delta_n=E|X_n-X|\to0 and Xn≽YX_n\succeq Y for every nn. The Lipschitz property gives

|vXn(t)−vX(t)|≤δnfor every t.|v_{X_n}(t)-v_X(t)|\le\delta_n\quad\text{for every }t.

Fix ε>0\varepsilon>0, and choose nn with δn<ε/2\delta_n<\varepsilon/2. On a large set, vXn−vY≥−ε/2v_{X_n}-v_Y\ge-\varepsilon/2, hence vX−vY≥−εv_X-v_Y\ge-\varepsilon. This is precisely (1) for X≽YX\succeq Y. The proof verifies the topic’s upper-section clause on the full domain, including nonintegrable XX and YY.

Shifts

For each fixed real bb and arbitrary XX,

qt(X+b)−qt(X)→b,|qt(X+b)−qt(X)|≤|b|.q_t(X+b)-q_t(X)\longrightarrow b,\qquad |q_t(X+b)-q_t(X)|\le |b|.

Dominated convergence gives vX+b−vX→bv_{X+b}-v_X\to b. Simultaneously shifting X,YX,Y therefore changes their comparison function by a function tending to zero. This proves Shift Invariance, in both directions.

For 0<p<10<p<1, subtract the values of the two mixtures in Transfer of a Shift Across a Mixture:

vMp(X+b/p,Y)−vMp(X,Y+b/(1−p))=p(vX+b/p−vX)−(1−p)(vY+b/(1−p)−vY)→b−b=0.\begin{aligned} &v_{M_p(X+b/p,Y)}-v_{M_p(X,Y+b/(1-p))}\\ &\qquad=p(v_{X+b/p}-v_X) -(1-p)(v_{Y+b/(1-p)}-v_Y)\longrightarrow b-b=0. \end{aligned}

The mixtures are therefore indifferent. Neither shift conclusion has used reflection symmetry or finite individual expectations.

A symmetric Cauchy has positive value

Let CσC_\sigma have the symmetric Cauchy density σ/[π(σ2+x2)]\sigma/[\pi(\sigma^2+x^2)], where σ>0\sigma>0. Such a variable is available on the fixed atomless probability space. Direct integration over the window and its two clipped tails gives

$$v_{C_\sigma}(t)= \frac{\sigma}{2\pi}\log\frac{\sigma^2+4t^2}{\sigma^2+t^2} +\frac{2t}{\pi}\arctan\frac{\sigma}{2t} -\frac{t}{\pi}\arctan\frac{\sigma}{t} \longrightarrow \frac{\sigma\log2}{\pi}.\tag{3}$$

Indeed the logarithmic term tends to σlog2/π\sigma\log2/\pi, while the last two terms each tend in magnitude to σ/π\sigma/\pi and cancel. Put k=log2/π>0k=\log2/\pi>0. Equation (3) yields C1∼k≻0C_1\sim k\succ0.

  • Symmetric Gambles Are Neutral fails: C1C_1 and −C1-C_1 have the same law but $C_1\not\sim0$.

  • Folded Expectation fails: both C1C_1 and zero have identically zero combined symmetric tail difference, so that principle would require them to be indifferent.

Alternating St Petersburg is zero

Let AA take (−2)n(-2)^n with probability 2−n2^{-n}, n≥1n\ge1. Group consecutive odd and even indices. Each pair has a negative outcome −a-a of probability 2r2r and positive outcome 2a2a of probability rr, where a,r>0a,r>0. For every t>0t>0,

qt(−a)=−min(a,t),qt(2a)=2min(a,t),q_t(-a)=-\min(a,t),\qquad q_t(2a)=2\min(a,t),

Scope of the verification

This model establishes, among other separations, that DTU together with CDF-Area Extension, Relative Expectation, L¹ Continuity, and both shift principles still does not force symmetric neutrality or reflection. No scale or sum-invariance verdict has been inferred from this construction. The map can derive further failures from its separately proved incompatibility records.
