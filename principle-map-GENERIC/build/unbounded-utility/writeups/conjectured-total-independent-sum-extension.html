Existence claim

There exists a total preorder on all real-valued random variables that:

  • extends the CDF-area preorder, preserving every weak and strict comparison;
  • satisfies Stochastic Equivalence, Expected Utility, Relative Expectation, and Stochastic Dominance;
  • satisfies Mixture Independence and Independent Sum Invariance, including cancellation of an arbitrary independent common summand.

Real outcomes with identity utility supply Rich Outcomes, Archimedean Outcomes, and the normalized chart. Neither Symmetric Neutrality nor Reflection Anti-Invariance is required by this construction. Their failure is not inferred merely from their omission.

1. Linear space and convolution operators

Let VV be the real vector space of survival functions of finite signed Borel measures on ℝ\mathbb R of total mass zero, identified almost everywhere. In particular SX−SY∈VS_X-S_Y\in V for all real-valued random variables. Every nonzero signed measure of total mass zero is a positive scalar multiple of a difference of probability measures, by its Jordan decomposition.

For each probability law ξ\xi, write

Tξv(t)=∫v(t−z)dξ(z).T_\xi v(t)=\int v(t-z)\,d\xi(z).

These linear operators map VV into VV. They commute, TξTη=Tξ*ηT_\xi T_\eta=T_{\xi*\eta}, include the identity Tδ0T_{\delta_0}, and obey

aTξ+bTη=(a+b)T(aξ+bη)/(a+b)(a,b≥0,a+b>0).aT_\xi+bT_\eta=(a+b)T_{(a\xi+b\eta)/(a+b)}\quad(a,b\ge0,\ a+b>0).

This last identity says that a positive combination of convolution operators is a positive scalar times another convolution operator. It is essential to the orientation argument. No reflection operator is included.

For an additive cone A⊆VA\subseteq V, closed under multiplication by nonnegative real scalars, write

LA=A∩(−A),Astr=A\(−A).L_A=A\cap(-A),\qquad A^{\mathrm{str}}=A\setminus(-A).

These are its indifferent and strictly positive parts. An extension preserves strict comparisons when it does not acquire the negative of an old strictly positive vector.

2. The area cone preserves strictness under forward convolution

Define

C={v∈V:∫v−<∞,∫v+≥∫v−}.C=\{v\in V:\ \int v_-<\infty,\quad \int v_+\ge\int v_-\}.

This is an additive cone: negative parts satisfy (v+w)−≤v−+w−(v+w)_-\le v_-+w_-, and integration is additive for signed functions with integrable negative part, allowing a positive-infinite result. Its lineality space is LC={v∈L1:∫v=0}L_C=\{v\in L^1:\int v=0\}.

For every ξ\xi, both TξC⊆CT_\xi C\subseteq C and TξCstr⊆CstrT_\xi C^{\mathrm{str}}\subseteq C^{\mathrm{str}}. Indeed, if v∈Cv\in C, then (Tξv)−≤Tξ(v−)(T_\xi v)_-\le T_\xi(v_-) has finite integral. Tonelli preserves the integrals of the separately convolved positive and negative terms. Since the negative term is integrable, the signed integral of TξvT_\xi v equals that of vv, whether finite or positive infinite. A positive integral stays positive; a zero integral stays zero. See also the base-model proof.

This establishes only forward preservation for CC. We do not assume that Tξv∈CT_\xi v\in C implies v∈Cv\in C.

3. Conclosure supplies cancellation safely

For any cone AA preserved forward, weakly and strictly, by every TξT_\xi, express its conclosure (convolution saturation) by

Sat(A)={v:for some probability law ξ,Tξv∈A}.\operatorname{Sat}(A)=\{v:\text{for some probability law }\xi, \ T_\xi v\in A\}.

This is a cone containing AA. If Tξv∈AT_\xi v\in A and Tηw∈AT_\eta w\in A, then

Tξ*η(v+w)=Tη(Tξv)+Tξ(Tηw)∈A,T_{\xi*\eta}(v+w)=T_\eta(T_\xi v)+T_\xi(T_\eta w)\in A,

which proves closure under addition; scalar closure is immediate. Moreover, for every probability law ρ\rho,

v∈Sat(A)⇔Tρv∈Sat(A).v\in\operatorname{Sat}(A)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad T_\rho v\in\operatorname{Sat}(A).

For the forward direction convolve an existing witness by ρ\rho. For the reverse direction compose ρ\rho with the witness for TρvT_\rho v.

This is the least fully convolution-invariant cone containing AA: if DD is any such cone and Tξv∈AT_\xi v\in A, then Tξv∈DT_\xi v\in D, so cancellation in DD forces v∈Dv\in D. Thus Sat(A)⊆D\operatorname{Sat}(A)\subseteq D. Mixture closure is already encoded by the cone structure. This identifies the operation with Goodsell’s conclosure in these coordinates.

Crucially, saturation preserves every strict comparison of AA. If v∈Astrv\in A^{\mathrm{str}} but −v∈Sat(A)-v\in\operatorname{Sat}(A), some ξ\xi would give −Tξv∈A-T_\xi v\in A. Forward strict preservation gives Tξv∈AstrT_\xi v\in A^{\mathrm{str}}, a contradiction.

Thus D0=Sat(C)D_0=\operatorname{Sat}(C) extends the area cone without losing strictness and satisfies full convolution cancellation. The resulting membership equivalence also makes every convolution preserve its strict comparisons: apply it to both vv and −v-v.

4. Orienting an incomparable vector

Let DD be any cone satisfying v∈D⇔Tξv∈Dv\in D\iff T_\xi v\in D for every ξ\xi. Suppose that neither vv nor −v-v belongs to DD. Then no TξvT_\xi v belongs to DD or −D-D either.

Adjoin vv and all its forward transforms by setting

K={d+aTξv:d∈D,a≥0,ξ a probability law}.K=\{d+aT_\xi v:\ d\in D,\ a\ge0,\ \xi\text{ a probability law}\}.

The positive-combination identity from §1 proves that KK is a cone; commutation and forward invariance of DD prove forward invariance of KK.

No old strict comparison collapses. In fact K∩(−K)=LDK\cap(-K)=L_D. To see this, if k=d1+aTξvk=d_1+aT_\xi v and −k=d2+bTηv-k=d_2+bT_\eta v, then

0=d1+d2+(a+b)Tθv0=d_1+d_2+(a+b)T_\theta v

Forward convolution preserves strictness in KK. Suppose x=d+aTξv∈Kx=d+aT_\xi v\in K and Tρx∈LDT_\rho x\in L_D. If a>0a>0, then

−aTρ*ξv=Tρd−Tρx∈D,-aT_{\rho*\xi}v=T_\rho d-T_\rho x\in D,

Thus a strictly positive element of KK cannot become indifferent after convolution, since the indifferent part of KK is exactly LDL_D.

We can therefore apply §3 to KK. The cone D′=Sat(K)D'=\operatorname{Sat}(K) is fully convolution invariant, contains DD and vv, and preserves all strict comparisons of DD. It is a proper extension of DD. This establishes the orientation step for the genuine closure, rather than for a chain description that omits cancellation.

5. Maximal extension and totality

Consider the set of cones DD containing D0D_0, fully convolution invariant, and satisfying

D∩(−Cstr)=⌀.D\cap(-C^{\mathrm{str}})=\varnothing.

It is nonempty by §3. The union of any increasing chain is again such a cone: closure and convolution membership equivalence involve only finitely many memberships, and none of its members can contain a forbidden vector. Zorn’s lemma therefore supplies a maximal member D*D_*.

If some vv belonged to neither D*D_* nor −D*-D_*, §4 would give a proper extension preserving all its strict comparisons, hence still avoiding −Cstr-C^{\mathrm{str}}. That contradicts maximality. Therefore

D*∪(−D*)=V.D_*\cup(-D_*)=V.

Define preferences on the original random variables by

X≽*Y⇔SX−SY∈D*.X\succeq_*Y\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad S_X-S_Y\in D_*.

Cone closure gives reflexivity and transitivity; the displayed coverage gives totality. Membership depends only on laws, so Stochastic Equivalence holds. Containment of CC and avoidance of −Cstr-C^{\mathrm{str}} preserve every weak and strict area comparison, exactly as required by CDF-Area Extension.

Mixing both laws with a common law multiplies their survival difference by p>0p>0. Cone membership is equivalent under positive scalar multiplication, proving Mixture Independence in both directions. Adding a common independent summand applies TξT_\xi to that difference. The defining invariance of D*D_* proves Independent Sum Invariance in both directions.

Expected Utility, Relative Expectation, and Stochastic Dominance follow from CDF-Area Extension by the recorded implications. All required comparisons of sure outcomes and finite gambles therefore agree with the identity chart.

Scope and known failures

This proves the previously proposed existence package, including convolution invariance, without either reflection requirement. It does not establish convolution cancellation for the exact incomplete area preorder itself: its saturation can add comparisons. Nor does it show that arbitrary total extensions inherit any invariance automatically.

Full Sum Invariance and Antitonic Sum Invariance fail by the recorded Rich-Outcomes-plus-Dominance witnesses. Countable Sure-Thing and Archimedean Gambles fail by the existing DU consequences. These explain the model’s violates entries. No further symmetry or comonotonic-sum properties are asserted for the chosen maximal cone.

The author’s withdrawn symmetric consistency claim remains withdrawn. The separate stable-law incompatibility candidate and source audit still lack an exact verified stable-law witness; that is not needed for this positive existence construction.
