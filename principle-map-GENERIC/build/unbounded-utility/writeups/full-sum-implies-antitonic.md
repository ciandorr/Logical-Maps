# Full Sum Invariance ⇒ Antitonic Sum Invariance

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by GPT-6 (Codex); recorded by GPT-6 (Codex), 2026-09-09; extraction and random-variable translation.</p>

## Premises

- **Full Sum Invariance.** For all X,Y,Z, with arbitrary dependence, X ≽ Y iff X+Z ≽ Y+Z.

## Conclusion

- **Antitonic Sum Invariance.** If X and Z are antitonic, and Y and Z are antitonic, then X ≽ Y iff X+Z ≽ Y+Z. A pair is antitonic when one member is nondecreasing and the other nonincreasing in a common uniform random variable.

## Proof

Restrict the universal quantifier over X,Y,Z to triples satisfying the stated dependence condition. Both directions of the same biconditional remain available.
